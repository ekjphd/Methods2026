Turn your phone sideways to play IRB Land.
ENG 6812 · Week 13

Click the deck to draw a card.

How to play

  1. On your turn, click the deck. Your pawn moves to the next square of that color. Double cards move two squares of that color. Picture cards send you to a landmark.
  2. Your team has 30 seconds to answer. Right answer: stay on the new square. Wrong answer: go back.
  3. Start at the bottom and climb to IRB Castle at the top. The first team there wins.
